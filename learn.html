AWARENESS LIBRARY

Learn the basics of staying safe online.

01 · EMAIL

Phishing

Phishing is a deceptive attempt to steal information by pretending to be a trusted person or organization.

Warning signs

  • Unexpected attachments or links
  • Urgent threats or prize claims
  • Misspelled sender addresses or domains
  • Requests for passwords, OTPs, or payment

Safety tip: Visit the official website directly instead of clicking an unexpected link.

02 · DEVICES

Malware

Malware is software designed to disrupt devices, spy on users, or gain unauthorized access.

Protection habits

  • Install software from trusted sources
  • Keep your operating system updated
  • Use reputable security software
  • Avoid unknown USB drives and attachments

Safety tip: Back up important files regularly.

03 · ACCOUNTS

Password & MFA

Weak or reused passwords make it easier for attackers to access multiple accounts.

Good practices

  • Use long, unique passwords
  • Use a trusted password manager
  • Enable multi-factor authentication
  • Never share OTPs or recovery codes

Safety tip: A password manager helps create unique passwords for every account.

04 · NETWORKS

Public Wi-Fi

Open public networks may expose users to monitoring or malicious look-alike hotspots.

Stay safer

  • Confirm the correct network name with staff
  • Avoid sensitive transactions on unknown Wi-Fi
  • Turn off automatic connection
  • Use HTTPS and keep device sharing off

Safety tip: Do not assume a network is safe just because it has a familiar name.

05 · PEOPLE

Social Engineering

Attackers manipulate people into revealing information or performing unsafe actions.

Protect yourself

  • Verify unusual requests using another channel
  • Do not reveal confidential details over calls
  • Be careful with impersonation and urgency
  • Follow your institution's verification process

Safety tip: Pause and verify before acting on pressure.

06 · PRIVACY

Data Privacy

Personal data can be misused when it is shared publicly or collected unnecessarily.

Privacy habits

  • Review app permissions
  • Limit personal details on public profiles
  • Use privacy settings
  • Think before sharing photos or documents

Safety tip: Share only the information needed for a trusted purpose.
